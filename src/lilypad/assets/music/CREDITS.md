# Credits: bundled party anthems

Four original instrumental tracks, generated 2026-11-25 with Google Lyria 3 Pro (`google/lyria-3-pro-preview`,
via OpenRouter) from prompts written for this project, then pitch-shifted to C major with ffmpeg rubberband so they
sit in the keyboard's C-pentatonic scale. Beat maps were made with `tools/art/beatmap.py`.
AI-generated music; no samples or copyrighted material were supplied as input.
