### J10: gait frame schedules, painted Pip and pad, painted minis

- `animals.py`: each gait now picks its frame from the effect's own clock (`AnimalCrossing.pose_for`, no rng).
  Walkers alternate idle/step on every bob trough (2.6 per second), hoppers show `step` while `sin(pi * phase) > 0.25`
  and still squash on landing, flyers flap at 3 Hz, swimmers swish at 1.2 Hz and the jellyfish pulses at 1.5 Hz.
  A blink overrides all of them except a hop's landing squash. With traced or drawn art `step` aliases `idle`, so the
  legacy look is pixel-identical. The painted farm four already ride the painted route and keep their peekaboo.
- `critter.py`: `set_pip_art("painted" | "drawn", root=None)` selects the route. Painted Pip loads the manifest's
  `[pip]` frames (idle, blink, cheer, eyes_white) and `[pad]` into the same `(radius, pose)` caches, scaled into the
  drawn sprite's box (3.0 r tall) and the drawn pad's height (1.4 r), so `floor_y` and `pad_reach` are unchanged.
  `cheer` shows during the party dance and celebrations. `Frog.look(dx, dy)` draws two pupil discs over `eyes_white`
  (nothing calls it yet). Missing or invalid art logs once and falls back to the drawn Pip. The module default is
  `"drawn"`; `__main__` must call `set_pip_art(cfg.effects.pip_art, root=...)` before the engine builds the Frog.
- `animal_art.py`: count-along minis load from `[minis.<name>]` when the art mode is `painted`, else the drawn minis.
