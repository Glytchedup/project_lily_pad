- Storybook letter cards (`effects/storybook.py`): press the same letter three times within 1.2 s and the painted pond dissolves into that letter's full-screen card for 4 s, then dissolves back. In attract mode the cards cycle every 8 s. The second press prefetches the card on a single decode thread; a decode that is not ready within 1 s skips the show. Nothing is queued or hidden, so every key still draws on top of the card. Disabled (`effects.storybook = "off"`) it creates no thread and changes no pixel. The shipped manifest is empty until the painted JPEGs are added.
