### J20 — Living pond encounters and Pip's beat-synced hops

- Added a seeded Director that selects rides, ducks, jumps, waves and greetings from crossing positions. Encounters share a six-second cooldown and pause below a quality scale of 0.5.
- Walkers can pause and double-bounce while their bob and blink continue. A crossing can gain at most 1.5 seconds. Mirrored back landmarks follow the displayed pose.
- Pip can ride a moving back, hop off on an arrow press, duck, wave and track a target with his pupils. A ride expires after eight seconds or sooner if the animal leaves.
- A supplied party beat clock times each landing to the next mapped beat. The original dance still runs without a clock.
- INT2 connects these hooks to the ecosystem and party-show toggles.
